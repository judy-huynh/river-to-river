The record

Before reading the street as it is, it is worth remembering what it took to get here. The 42nd Street Development Project ran across four mayors and two real estate cycles. Lynne Sagalyn’s Times Square Roulette is the account of it.

What the street was for

Male turf. Sex shops, action movies, retail built around one appetite. The street was read nationally as the icon of urban distress, and the press and the film industry kept it that way.

What the plan was for

Sagalyn’s point is that the redemption was assembled, not discovered. Condemnation, subsidy, negotiated deals, and a coalition that had to be held together for two decades while the market and the mayors turned over.

      07

      How this was made

      The lots

      Every tax lot in NYC PLUTO whose centre falls within 125 feet of the 42nd Street centreline, plus any lot addressed on 42nd Street within 230 feet. 82 lots. The rule is mine, and section 06 says what that costs.

      The centreline

      Pulled from OpenStreetMap rather than interpolated. Measured at 10,411 feet, 1.97 miles, from 12th Avenue to 1st Avenue.

      Unbuilt floor area

      The greater of the residential, commercial and community facility allowance, minus what is built, times lot area. This is a screening figure. Ninety per cent of it sits on lots where a special district or a landmark designation overrides the base rule.

      What is not measured

      Sidewalk width, truck volumes and bus reliability are observations, not yet data. Sidewalk polygons, DOT truck routes and MTA bus speeds would close that gap.
