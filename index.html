What the data says

  • 3.4m sq ft of floor area the zoning appears to allow and nobody built
  • only 10% of that survives a check against special districts and landmarks
  • 88% of the capacity sits outside the four famous hubs
  • the largest single opening is a city owned lot at 234 West 42nd
  • 67% of the street has no drawn design on it
