The ribbon

The same street, kept in its real position, but tipped up and locked to its own bearing so it always runs left to right. The city is never shown north up, because nobody experiences 42nd Street from above. Buildings carry their real heights, so the four hubs appear as the canyons they are and the neglected stretches appear as the gaps they are.

Hudson River Bearing locked at 28.9° · buildings from Mapbox Streets

Heights only draw in close. The building data arrives around the zoom where you can read a block, so at the whole street framing the mass is sparse. Travel it, or zoom in, and the wall builds itself. What this does that a plan cannot. Height is the thing a planning map throws away first, and height is what you actually feel walking. Port Authority reads as a wall, Bryant Park reads as a hole in the wall, and the stretch west of 9th reads as almost nothing at all.
