42nd Street, graded

The ribbon geometry with the void palette, built on the Mapbox Standard style rather than a hand rolled one. The whole city is pushed through a colour lookup table I generated, so the map is graded like film instead of styled layer by layer. Move the light around the day, turn the weather on, and click any building in the city to interrogate it.

  Standard style · custom 32³ LUT · interactions API

What is actually new here. setColorTheme pushes every pixel of the basemap through a 32³ lookup table, so the palette is a colour grade rather than a stylesheet. setConfigProperty moves the sun, which is why the shadows change and not just the colours. addInteraction makes every building in the Standard style clickable without adding a single layer of my own. setRain and setSnow put weather in the scene. line-trim-offset draws the street onto the map as you travel it. New York is one of the cities with real landmark models, so Grand Central and the Chrysler arrive as themselves rather than as grey boxes.
